### 1. Matrix-Vector Product $Ax$

To compute $Ax$:

$$
Ax = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} (2 \times 4) + (-1 \times 2) \\ (1 \times 4) + (3 \times 2) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 - 2 \\ 4 + 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ 10 \end{pmatrix}
$$

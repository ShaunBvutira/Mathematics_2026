### 1. Matrix Computations

#### **Product AB**

$$
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

* **Row 1, Column 1:** $(1 \times 2) + (2 \times 3) = 2 + 6 = 8$
* **Row 1, Column 2:** $(1 \times 0) + (2 \times 1) = 0 + 2 = 2$
* **Row 2, Column 1:** $(0 \times 2) + (1 \times 3) = 0 + 3 = 3$
* **Row 2, Column 2:** $(0 \times 0) + (1 \times 1) = 0 + 1 = 1$

### Exercise 3. Ellipse

For the equation

$$\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$$

find the semi-axis lengths, the intercepts with the coordinate axes, and make a sketch. Explain along which axis the ellipse is more stretched and how this is visible in the equation.

---

### Solution

#### Step 1. Determine the semi-axis lengths

Comparing $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$ with the canonical equation of an ellipse centered at the origin:

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$$

1. **Horizontal semi-axis ($a$):**
   $$a^2 = 25 \implies a = \sqrt{25} = 5$$

2. **Vertical semi-axis ($b$):**
   $$b^2 = 9 \implies b = \sqrt{9} = 3$$

Since $a = 5$ and $b = 3$, the major semi-axis length is $5$ and the minor semi-axis length is $3$.

---

#### Step 2. Find the intercepts with the coordinate axes

- **$x$-intercepts (set $y = 0$):**
  $$\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{0^2}{9} = 1 \implies x^2 = 25 \implies x = \pm 5$$
  The $x$-intercepts are $(5, 0)$ and $(-5, 0)$ (the vertices).

- **$y$-intercepts (set $x = 0$):**
  $$\frac{0^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1 \implies y^2 = 9 \implies y = \pm 3$$
  The $y$-intercepts are $(0, 3)$ and $(0, -3)$ (the co-vertices).

---

#### Step 3. Focal Distance and Foci Calculation

The distance $c$ from the center to each focus satisfies $c^2 = a^2 - b^2$:

$$c = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = \sqrt{16} = 4$$

The foci lie along the major axis ($x$-axis) at $F_1(-4, 0)$ and $F_2(4, 0)$.

#### Step 5. Axis stretching explanation

- **Which axis is more stretched:** The ellipse is **more stretched along the $x$-axis** (horizontal direction).

- **How it is visible in the equation:**
  Look directly at the denominators beneath the squared variables in the canonical equation $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$:
  - The denominator under $x^2$ is $25$.
  - The denominator under $y^2$ is $9$.
